## Implemented operating design

- **Online:** trusted Neon cart validation → durable attempt and reservations → idempotent Stripe session creation → verified paid session → atomic order/items/inventory/outbox commit. Unknown financial outcomes retain reservations for explicit reconciliation. A cancelled browser return does not prove a session expired.
- **Recovery:** administrators inspect unresolved attempts in Orders and reconcile against Stripe. Unrelated Stripe account events do not create Plankz orders. Inconsistent payments remain visible rather than being assigned to guessed products.
- **Catalogue:** published validated products use the existing commerce URLs. Boards have a single tracked unit; merchandise stock has an explicit unit and variant. Drafts, archived records, photography-only entries and unavailable stock cannot enter checkout. Historical orders retain their snapshots.
- **Markets:** take all event stock offline first, including every displayed unique deck and the whole merchandise product when its stock will be shared at the event. Resolve existing online reservations before physical payment. Record the successful native contactless payment reference, explicit products/quantities, actual line totals, operator and sale time. One succeeded AUD card-present payment becomes one collected market order.
- **Market exceptions:** unknown/mismatched/unavailable payments and stock conflicts create durable `needs_review` records without paid orders or stock decrement. Affected products cannot be republished or adjusted until resolved. Correct/retry the record, or dismiss an evidenced duplicate/mistake with actor, reason and timestamp; dismissal never changes stock.
- **Refunds:** use the business Stripe Dashboard. Synchronize cumulative refund details into administration and expose refresh failures. A refund, dispute or pickup cancellation never silently restores inventory. Restock only after a deliberate stock adjustment with a reason.
- **Email:** order and pickup messages use configured SMTP, stable identities and a durable notification record. Failed delivery remains visible and retryable. Provider acceptance is distinct from inbox delivery. Market customers may omit email.
